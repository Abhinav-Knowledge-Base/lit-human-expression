# Beautiful Khayal

I am trying to map the territory of my own joy,
to understand what it is that sets my chest on fire.

Is it the wild, untamed scripts my mind writes at midnight?
Or the quiet, sudden gravity that pulls me back to you
the moment I see a stranger cross the street?

I have built a cathedral out of your absence.
Inside it, I don't just remember you—
I recreate you.
I hold entire dialogues with the version of you I keep inside me,
asking questions you never heard,
receiving answers you never gave.

I watch the world happen around me,
and like a shadow director, I rewrite the scenes:
If you were here, I would have held your hand like this.
If you were here, the rain would taste like this.

It is a beautiful, exhausting war.
This conflict inside me—
this ache that feels half like a bruise and half like a prize.

Am I happy because of who you are,
or am I happy because of who I become when I think of you?
Perhaps it doesn't matter.

Whether it is the dream, the memory, or the madness,
the truth is simpler:

You are the spark,
but this universe of longing—
this beautiful khayal—
belongs entirely to me.
