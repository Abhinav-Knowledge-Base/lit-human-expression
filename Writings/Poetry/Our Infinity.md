# Our Infinity

They say between any two numbers
lives an infinity —
and between two larger ones,
a larger infinity still.

But mathematicians will tell you,
with a quiet kind of wonder,
that no infinity outruns another.
They are all, somehow, equal.

And so it is with us.

The moments we have lived together
are infinite in their own right —
laughter folded into ordinary Tuesdays,
silence that never needed filling,
the small eternities of your hand in mine.

I will not mourn the infinity we didn't get.
I will not measure ours against a longer dream.

Instead, I'll count what cannot be counted —
the love that kept renewing itself,
the care that never asked for tallying,
the emotions that spilled past every border
we thought we had.

Cheers to this —
our strange, magnificent, uncountable infinity.

Not the biggest.
Not the smallest.

Just ours.

And that,
as it turns out,
is more than enough.
